## The daily drill (~60–90 min solo, while you still have full days)

A repeatable loop — do it every morning before anything else:

1. **Today review (15–20 min).** Clear the due queue, including the energy deck. Swipe to rate.
2. **Read one leveled article (15 min).** Use the Browse tab (DW Top-Thema / Nachrichtenleicht / Tagesschau leicht are wired in). Save 5–8 words *deliberately*.
3. **Grammar focus (15 min).** One target area per day — rotate Konjunktiv II → Passiv → Kasus/Deklination → Konnektoren. Generate a Cloze or Grammar set; the profile already steers it toward these.
4. **Output (15–20 min) — the weak side.** Write 6–10 sentences using today's words *and* today's grammar, then open Discuss and talk about the article in German.
5. **Optional listening (10 min).** DW *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten*.
