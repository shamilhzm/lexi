# The second panel — 2026-10-04

The owner asked for "a circle of experts … the 10 best app developers in the world in
dialogue with one another" to review what was built on 2026-10-02 (Focus and Hören) and
to chart a path forward.

**The ten are composite archetypes, not real people.** Each seat describes the kind of
work its holder has shipped, and no real developer's views are being quoted.

**Method.** Each seat wrote a first read (Round 1) from the code, the docs, the live
build and the measurements below. They then argued the points they disagreed on
(Round 2), and the consensus became the path forward. **Nothing here is built yet.**
Open work is tracked at the top of [BACKLOG](BACKLOG.md).

## Round 1 — first reads

**Audio.**
- The one-file render is the right call for a web page. The one thing iOS reliably lets
  keep playing is an element that is already playing, and it held through a lock on the
  simulator.
- Its cost is memory. Uncompressed 16 kHz WAV is about 1.9 MB a minute
  (16,000 samples × 2 bytes × 60), so the one-hour option holds ~115 MB in a Blob.
- When the clips are hosted, skip rendering entirely. **MP3 or ADTS-AAC frames
  concatenate into a valid stream**: lay pre-encoded clips and pre-encoded silences end
  to end and you have the programme in milliseconds. That means no decode, no
  OfflineAudioContext, and an eighth of the memory.
- Thorsten and Cori are not level-matched, and on a kitchen counter you hear it.
  Normalise every clip to one speech loudness.

**Habits.**
- You built the best passive feature in the category and gave it no trigger.
- Lexi has no way to bring anyone back: no notifications (undecided), and no widget (a
  widget needs native code). The ready slot is a trigger only for someone already inside
  the app.
- Retention is a trigger problem before it is a content problem. "Hören at 8:00,
  tomorrow's words are ready" is the single highest-value notification this app could
  send.

**Memory.**
- Hören's shape is the best version of passive I know of: expanding returns, the English
  faded out, a sentence for context.
- **It leaks the test.** It plays the day's *due* words, so a learner who listens over
  breakfast hears the answer minutes before Üben asks for it. FSRS then credits that
  borrowed memory and lengthens the interval.
  - New words are different. They have no schedule to corrupt, and hearing them first
    is real learning.
  - Due words should wait until the day's reviews are done.
- **It cannot be evaluated.** By design it records nothing, and this repo's own LESSONS
  has an entry titled *A mechanic with no observable output cannot be evaluated*.
- **One voice is a missed effect.** Learners who hear new words in several voices
  (talker variability) learn them better (Barcroft & Sommers 2005), and they recognise
  them from new speakers.

**iOS.**
- The simulator is not the phone for background audio. The real questions are:
  - Low Power Mode;
  - memory pressure while locked;
  - Safari tab versus home-screen web app, which have historically behaved differently.

  The owner's phone, in both modes, is the test that decides.
- **Memory while locked.** Under memory pressure, iOS ends the heaviest background work
  first. After preparing, this page still holds onnxruntime's heap with two 63 MB voices
  in it, *plus* the WAV. Run synthesis in a Worker and terminate the Worker when
  preparation ends. WebAssembly memory never shrinks, so ending the Worker is the reliable
  way to hand it back.
- **Durability.** Hören adds ~200 MB to the origin (138.1 MB of voices, plus a clip
  cache of up to 60 MB). Safari evicts an origin whole, so the learner's history now
  shares a fate with a rebuildable cache. Ask for persistent storage in the same tap as
  the consent.
- **A native shell** (AVAudioSession `.playback`) is the reliable form of everything
  Hören does. It also brings local notifications without a server, and storage that is
  never evicted. STORES.md already names its preconditions.

**Perf.**
- The entry is at 94% of its budget, and Hören's whole engine is in it although none of
  it is needed for first paint.
- Split it: a tiny state module the top bar can read, and the engine, sheet and Practise
  aloud behind a dynamic import on the first tap.
- Synthesis runs on the main thread, so scrolling during preparation will jank. A Worker
  fixes that as well as the memory problem.
- `store.ts` is 2,197 lines. Split it by concern the next time it is touched, not before.

**Design.**
- **The button.** 🎧 in the top bar is the right place. Icon-only is ambiguous, though:
  to some it reads as "audio settings". Teach it once, with a first-run hint that names
  it *Hören*.
- **The mini-player.** Stop (✕) sits beside skip, so one slip ends the programme. Move
  stop into the sheet and keep the bar to word, save and play/pause.
- **The sheet.** It closes the moment consent turns into preparing, which is abrupt;
  keep it open with the progress bar. Its back label, *where you were*, is a
  placeholder.
- **The top bar.** It fits at 320 px with 8 px to spare for a new learner. The streak
  chip has not been measured there.
- **The copy.** Hören's copy is the best in the app: warm and true. That is the voice
  Trails should be written in.

**Progression.**
- TRAILS.md has the right bones: stamps backed by a check, absolute ranks, no leagues.
- Build one trail and watch whether people finish it. Designing badges without
  completion data is guessing.
- A Dackel is a good mascot if it appears at milestones and nowhere else.
- Never give points for listening minutes. Points must mean recall.

**Local-first.**
- Durability got heavier (see iOS). Two things belong in the same tap as Hören's
  consent: the backup nudge, before the first download, and `persist()`.
- Sync is still the biggest open architecture call (VISION open decision 1). Decide it
  after the domain, because a sync design is keyed to an origin too.

**Speech.**
- Render at build time, not on phones. At build time model size does not matter, so use
  the best open German voices there are, more than one, loudness-normalised, as MP3
  packs per level.
- **Gate the audio like the corpus.** Every clip passes an ASR round-trip: transcribe the
  clip, compare the transcript to its text, and reject on a mismatch. That catches
  garbled synthesis and mispronounced loanwords before a learner ever hears them.
- German Commons is the right source for examples. Its YouTube Commons transcripts are
  the spoken register the corpus lacks. Rejecting `de_audios_new` was correct.

**Founder.**
- **Velocity.** It is rare: `src/` rewritten almost entirely in a month, with 1,864 tests
  standing.
- **Direction churn.** There were three reframes in four weeks. Each was argued well,
  and each was argued from one learner.
- **The fix.** Freeze the shape and put Lexi in ten movers' hands before the next
  reframe.
- **Name and domain** block the stores, sharing, the native shell and durability.
  Decide them this month.
- **The Prototype Fund window** is open until 30 November 2026 (COSTS.md). Apply.
- **The docs** are the agents' memory, which is worth keeping. A 2,727-line backlog that
  calls itself unreconciled has stopped being a backlog, though. Archive it.

## Round 2 — the dialogue

### 1. Does Hören force a native app now?

> **iOS:** Hören is exactly the feature a web page does worst. Build the Capacitor shell now.
>
> **Founder:** Not on the wrong origin. A shell runs on its own storage, so ship it before
> the name and domain are settled and you strand the only data that matters — the owner's.
>
> **Audio:** And not before we know the web version fails. If the owner's phone plays
> thirty locked minutes from the home screen, the web is good enough for this month.
>
> **Habits:** The shell's real argument isn't audio, it's the notification.

**Settled.** The web stays canonical, and the owner's device test sets the urgency.

- **If Hören fails locked on a real iPhone,** the shell moves to *Next*, with audio as
  its first job.
- **If it holds,** the shell comes after the domain, justified by local notifications.

### 2. Make the audio on the phone, or host it?

> **Speech:** Host it. Pre-render at build time with better voices, gate every clip, ship MP3 packs.
>
> **Local-first:** Every fetch is a request a learner makes to a third party. Per-level packs,
> so nothing reveals which words a learner is on.
>
> **Audio:** Then the client never renders: concatenate frames into one file, same lock-proof
> property, a fraction of the memory.
>
> **Perf:** Keep on-device synthesis for cards newer than the last pack — in a Worker you
> terminate.

**Settled.** Hosted MP3 packs per level, concatenated on the device. On-device synthesis
stays as the fallback, in a terminating Worker.

### 3. "Records nothing" — or a mechanic you can evaluate?

> **Memory:** Your own LESSONS says a mechanic with no observable output cannot be evaluated.
> Right now nobody can say whether Hören teaches anything.
>
> **Local-first:** Fine, if it is the learner's data, on the learner's device, and in their backup.
>
> **Design:** And shown to them. "Words you heard in Hören and later recalled in Üben: *x*% —
> words you didn't hear: *y*%" is the most motivating sentence this app could print, if it's true.
>
> **Habits:** And if it isn't true, you'll want to know that even more.

**Settled — needs a VISION amendment.**

- Keep a private *heard* log (id and time). It is never an FSRS write and never a dwell
  exposure, it goes in the backup, and it exists to compare recall.
- Fortschritt shows the comparison only once each group has enough reviews to mean
  something.

### 4. Due words in Hören

> **Memory:** Hearing the answer before the test inflates the test.
>
> **Habits:** But hearing words you're about to forget is exactly what keeps them.
>
> **Memory:** Yes — *after* the review. Same words, different order, no leak.

**Settled.** The order becomes:

1. saved words;
2. new words, where hearing them first is honest pre-exposure;
3. words already reviewed today;
4. due words, only once today's reviews are done.

### 5. Does listening count toward the goal or the streak?

> **Habits:** Count it. People keep what's counted.
>
> **Progression:** Never as points. Points mean recall.
>
> **Memory:** Minutes aren't learning.
>
> **Founder:** Then show minutes as minutes.

**Settled.** *Listened this week*, in minutes, sits on Fortschritt as a habit stat. It is
never points, never level, and never the streak, which keeps meaning *you practised*.

### 6. Trails — now or later?

> **Progression:** One trail, soon, or the proposal goes stale.
>
> **iOS:** No new surfaces until Hören is proven on a phone.
>
> **Founder:** Both: prove Hören this week, then one trail — Behörden — as the next thing,
> and judge it by whether real learners finish it.

**Settled.** One vertical slice comes after the *Now* list: Behörden, three units, a
check, a stamp and the first rank. It waits on the owner's choices in TRAILS.md.

### 7. Is "vocabulary only" the right one thing?

> **Memory:** Yes — but at the Bürgeramt the unit people need is often a chunk: *Ich hätte gern
> einen Termin.* A chunk is vocabulary.
>
> **Founder:** And that's the bridge to Trails. Scenes are built of words *and* phrases.

**Settled.** Grammar stays hidden. Phrases become first-class cards in trails, gated the
way the fixed phrases already shipped (*Wie spät ist es?*) are.

### 8. Velocity versus churn

> **Founder:** Three reframes in four weeks, each from one learner's experience.
>
> **Perf:** The tests held through all of it. Velocity isn't the problem.
>
> **Founder:** Direction is. Every reframe resets what a learner expects to find.

**Settled.** The shape is frozen for four weeks. Allowed in that time: bug fixes, Hören
hardening and the one trail slice. The next reframe needs evidence from at least five
learners who are not the owner.

### 9. No telemetry — so how does Lexi learn anything?

> **Habits:** You can't improve what you can't see.
>
> **Local-first:** And VISION refuses telemetry, rightly.
>
> **Founder:** Then ask people. Ten learners, a three-question check-in each week, and an
> opt-in "send my week" button that composes the numbers into a message the learner reads
> before sending.

**Settled.**

- No telemetry, as VISION says.
- On-device numbers the learner can see.
- A user-initiated weekly summary.
- Interviews.

## What all ten agreed on

1. **Hören is the right answer to the owner's ask.** Prove it on a real iPhone, in both
   Safari and the home-screen app, before building on it.
2. **Fix the due-word leak now.** It quietly corrupts the scheduler, which is the one
   asset everything else stands on.
3. **Make Hören measurable, privately.** Otherwise its value is a belief.
4. **Hosted, gated audio is the endgame.** On-device synthesis is the fallback.
5. **Decide the name and domain this month.** Four other decisions wait on them.
6. **Ten real learners before the next reframe.**
7. **Keep the honesty, keep moving it into mechanics,** and write everything else the way
   Hören's sheet is written.

## What not to touch

- **The corpus and its machine gate.**
- **FSRS, and grading only on a deliberate act.**
- **Local-first by default.**
- **The test culture.** Mutation-checked guards, budgets, and LESSONS before work.
- **The single playing file in the background.** Hosted MP3 concatenation keeps that
  same property.
- **Hören's copy.**

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Audio:** The right architecture for the web. Host the clips and concatenate frames;
  stop rendering on phones.
- **Habits:** A great feature without a trigger. A notification is worth more than a new
  surface.
- **Memory:** The best passive design going. Fix the due-word leak and measure it.
- **iOS:** Simulator-proven, phone-unproven. Free the memory before the lock, and keep
  the shell ready for the day the phone says no.
- **Perf:** Healthy and well tested, but the entry is nearly full. Lazy-load Hören.
- **Design:** Hören's copy is the app's new voice. Fix the mini-player's stop and teach
  the icon.
- **Progression:** Trails has the right bones. Build one and count completions.
- **Local-first:** Durability got heavier. Ask for persistence when Hören asks for space.
- **Speech:** Gate the audio like the corpus. Better voices belong at build time, not on
  the phone.
- **Founder:** Ship velocity, steady direction. Name, domain, ten learners and the grant,
  this month.
