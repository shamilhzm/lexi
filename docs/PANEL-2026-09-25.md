# The panel review — 2026-09-25/26

Ten reviewers critiqued Lexi, answered each other, and implemented the consensus. This
is the record of *what they argued and how it was settled*; what shipped is in the
CHANGELOG ("Panel review"), and what is still open is at the top of the BACKLOG.

**The ten seats:** Germanist/DaF · product design & IA · UI/UX & accessibility ·
engagement & learning science · mobile engineering · law (German/EU) · marketing ·
finance · app stores · go-to-market.

**Method.** Round 1: each wrote an independent critique from the code, the docs and
eleven phone screenshots of seeded learners (`?seed=cold`, `b1`, `backlog`). Round 2:
each read the other nine and wrote a reply — what they co-sign, what they object to and
the resolution they would accept, and a revised top five. Round 3: a plan with file
ownership per reviewer; each built their rows on a branch, merged into `panel`.

## What every reviewer agreed on

1. **Data loss first.** The card map could be overwritten by a shorter one before it was
   read, after a degraded boot, or after a restore. All ten put this ahead of their own
   items. *Shipped* — `store.ts` write guard, five tests.
2. **Only a deliberate act counts.** The streak counted app opens (a cold visitor saw a
   flame); a swipe on the Üben card graded. Both now require a deliberate press.
3. **One honest new-word budget.** It reset on every Üben tap and stalled at 20 due. Now
   per day, saved words let through (up to 5), a backlog closes the day honestly.
4. **Public surfaces must not lie.** Stale counts ("6,500 words") in the page head,
   "costs cents" for the tutor, "nothing leaves your device" — replaced with numbers
   stamped at build or measured by a script, and a legal page listing every data flow.
5. **PWA first; a name and a domain before any launch.** Progress lives per origin, so
   the domain is the one decision that is expensive to change later.

## The disagreements, and how they were settled

| Question | Positions | Settled |
|---|---|---|
| **Walk mode: may it grade?** | Engagement, product: a headphone press after the spoken answer is a deliberate grade. GTM, marketing: listen-only first, grade in v2. UI/UX: grades provisional until the next unlock. Mobile: no speech recognition, ever. | Both lanes shipped: practice grades on a press (no press, no grade, one write per card per walk), listening never grades. Speech is never scored. The owner asked for "counts toward my progress", and a press is the same deliberate act as a button. UI/UX's confirmation step waits for the real-device test. |
| **Illustrations: style and colour** | Marketing, product: Aicher-style pictograms, gender-coloured. UI/UX: monochrome ink, never coloured by gender (colour would answer the gender drill). | Monochrome, the page's ink; gender ink off. Settled by a constraint as much as taste: no free image model exists, so pictograms are LLM-drawn SVG on a strict grid, which also makes them tiny, recolourable and CC0. |
| **Native app?** | App stores (round 1): Capacitor with the data inside. Mobile, finance, GTM: PWA first. | App stores reversed itself in round 2: a Capacitor app has its own storage, so existing learners would open an empty app. Web first; a Play TWA (same storage) next; iOS after an export/import handoff. |
| **Rebalance by frequency rank?** | A mechanical rule: promote every B1+ card ranked ≤ 1,500 to A2. | Refused by the Germanist after reading ~190 cards: wrong about 85% of the time (newspaper words rank high; nouns inherit ranks from same-spelled verbs). 26 promotions ruled one by one instead. |
| **Stories in store builds** | Legal, app stores: SRF/DW pages are fetched whole without written permission — fine for a free web app, not for a store listing (Apple 5.2.2). | `VITE_STORE_BUILD` keeps stories (and the tutor and the support link) out of store copies until publishers agree in writing. |
| **Split `cards.json` per level?** | Finance: yes, for bandwidth. Mobile: no — a hashed filename and a small first-paint file instead. | Not split; the size budget in CI holds it (421 KB of 480 KB). |

## Reviewer verdicts in one line each

- **Germanist:** the vocabulary is broad and machine-checked; recall marked correct German
  wrong, 299 nouns had no plural, and the drills tested rules more than words. *Fixed the
  first two; the drill redesign is open.*
- **Product design:** the loop did not close — nothing on the front door said saved words
  were waiting, and one swipe meant opposite things on two surfaces. *Ready slot, one
  gesture grammar.*
- **UI/UX:** the Üben card clipped its own content and read as one button to VoiceOver;
  four colours failed AA. *Rebuilt card, contrast guarded by a test.*
- **Engagement:** a two-week lapse could wipe a learner; the streak flattered; the recap
  brought the backlog back. *Durability surfaced, honest streak and forecast.*
- **Mobile:** progress could be overwritten; boot and deploy had no timeouts; the offline
  cache only grew. *Guard, watchdog, precache and prune, CSP, size budgets.*
- **Law:** no Impressum or privacy notice; attribution invisible in the app; the tutor sent
  text without consent. *Legal page, consent gate, licences split — Impressum awaits the
  owner.*
- **Marketing:** the name is crowded; public surfaces showed the retired product.
  *Counts stamped at build, README, share card with a link back.*
- **Finance:** hosting is ~€0 until heavy media ships; pre-rendered audio would not fit.
  *COSTS.md from a measuring script; walk mode renders on the phone instead.*
- **App stores:** "Lexi" cannot be the store name; no privacy policy; the manifest was
  incomplete. *Icons, manifest, store flag, listing text with a length test.*
- **Go-to-market:** a card-quality report could not reach the owner; nothing measured a
  launch. *Report to GitHub from every word sheet; `?ref=` tags read only in host logs.*
