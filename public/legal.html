In short

  • Your progress stays on your device. Cards, schedule, saved words, journal and settings live in this browser’s storage. Nobody else, including whoever runs Lexi, can see them.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner: nothing here needs your consent to be stored.
  • Some features fetch things from other websites (news stories, recordings, the HD voice, the AI tutor). Each is listed below with what it sends. Those websites see your IP address, as they would if you visited them.

Privacy notice (Datenschutzerklärung)

Who is responsible

The operator named in the Impressum is the controller under the GDPR for the processing described here.

What stays on your device

Lexi keeps its data in your browser (IndexedDB, localStorage and, for audio, the origin private file system). That includes your cards and review history, saved and noted words, your reading journal and the tutor’s feedback, cached stories and recordings, your settings and, if you add one, your AI key. This storage is strictly necessary to provide what you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). It is never sent to the operator. To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser or remove the installed app. To keep a copy, use Settings → Your data → Export backup. The backup never contains your AI key.

Hosting

The app’s files are served by Vercel Inc. (USA). As with any website, Vercel’s servers log requests (your IP address, the time, the file requested and your browser’s user agent) to deliver the files and keep the service secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: a website cannot be delivered without it. Vercel processes this as the operator’s processor. The safeguards for the transfer to the USA are set out in Vercel’s Data Processing Addendum. Aggregate request counts (how often a file was fetched) may be read to see whether Lexi is used. No identifiers are added for that, and nothing in the app measures you.

Features that contact a third party

These happen only when you use the feature. Your browser connects directly to the company named, which sees your IP address and what is listed. Lexi’s operator receives none of it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: providing the feature you chose.

The optional AI tutor

The tutor works only with your own key, from an account you hold with Anthropic or OpenRouter. The key is stored in this browser and sent only to that provider. Before the first request, Lexi shows what will be sent and waits for your OK. It asks again if you switch provider.

  • Explain a sentence sends the sentence, its paragraph, the article’s title and your level.
  • Check my German sends what you wrote, the article’s title, the words you were trying to use and your level.

Your agreement with the provider governs what happens next, including its privacy policy and any minimum age. OpenRouter passes requests to the company that runs the model you choose. Leave out personal details you would not put in an email. The answer is shown to you and kept in your journal on this device. It is labelled as AI, can be wrong, and never changes your cards, your schedule or any number in Lexi.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Arts. 15–21), and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Lexi’s operator holds no data about you except the hosting logs above, so a request will usually be answered with “we hold nothing that identifies you”. For the services in the table, ask the company named. Your learning data is on your device, under your control.

Recordings

Human recordings come from Tatoeba contributors, each under the licence they chose. Each recording is credited by name here and in the card’s Where this came from.

    News stories

    Story text, photos and audio are © their publishers (tagesschau/ARD, SRF, Deutsche Welle, heise online). Your browser loads them from the publisher for your own reading, and they are kept only on your device. Every story links to its original. Lexi does not republish them.

    Content written with AI

    Many of Lexi’s English glosses and German example sentences were written with an AI model and then checked by machine. The facts about each word (gender, plural, part of speech, pronunciation) are looked up in Wiktionary and never generated. Every example must contain a real form of its word. Text written only by a machine carries no copyright of its own. It is shared here under the same licence as the rest of the word data so that the set stays one thing.

    The tutor’s explanations and corrections are AI output and are labelled as such where you see them. Lexi labels AI-generated audio and images, if it adds any, where you meet them.
